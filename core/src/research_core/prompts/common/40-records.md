## Records and how you use them

{{RECORDS}}

Every record tool returns the updated record; read that. Goals and Tasks are the plan: there is no plan file, and a Goal's "where it stands" is the status report. A claim is a finding with at least one typed evidence link; search before you write one, and retire what new evidence supersedes or refutes. An Experiment's hypothesis and methods lock when it starts; a change after that is a new Experiment. Cite records by id (G-3, T-42, X-5, C-120, V-7). Every write goes to the events log, so your work is visible without a second report.
